How to set video as wallpaper on your PC

Updated October 30, 2026

Windows lets you pick a picture for your desktop, but not a clip. A free portable app fills that gap.

Set video as wallpaper on Windows 11

The standard background settings have no video option, so you need a small app that plays the clip behind your icons. This one is free and needs no installation.

  1. Download the ZIP from SourceForge, extract it and run the app.
  2. Drag your video onto the window to set it. You can also click Add Wallpaper and choose the file.
  3. The video starts playing behind your desktop icons.

The steps are the same if you want to set a video as wallpaper on Windows 10.

Turn your own clip into live wallpaper

You do not need a special wallpaper file. A clip you recorded or saved works, which is the simplest way to make live wallpaper on a PC. Short clips that loop cleanly tend to look best, because the jump back to the start is harder to notice.

What happens during games and on battery

When you launch a fullscreen game or a laptop runs on battery, playback pauses by itself and CPU and GPU usage drops near zero.

With more than one display, multi-monitor support keeps the moving wallpaper from being limited to a single screen.

Other things you can set

The same steps work for an animated image. Read how to set a GIF as wallpaper, or see everything the app can do.

Common questions

Can Windows 11 set a video as wallpaper without an app?

The standard background settings offer a picture, a solid color, a slideshow or Spotlight, with no video option. A separate app is the usual way to do it.

Does a video wallpaper slow down my PC?

Playback is hardware accelerated and pauses during fullscreen games and on battery, keeping CPU and GPU usage near zero while paused.

Can I use the same video as a screensaver?

Yes. Any wallpaper can also run as your screensaver.

Does this work on Windows 10?

Yes. The steps are the same on Windows 10 and Windows 11.
